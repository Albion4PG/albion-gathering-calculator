Albion Gathering Fame Calculator

Estimated fame/hour per zone if you skip any node worth less than a chosen minimum value. That minimum is the chart's x-axis: each point is "fame/hour if I only take nodes worth at least this much," swept across every real value found in that zone — pickier (further right) means fewer nodes qualify each run, but every one you do take is worth more.

Accounts for real per-charge harvest time (from the game's own tick rates), not just travel time and mob-kill time. One line per zone added to the chart.
